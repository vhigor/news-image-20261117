Ukraine • Russia — the war at sea

KERCH BRIDGE HIT: ROAD SPAN FALLS INTO THE STRAIT

Ukraine strikes the Crimea link for a second straight day — the road is severed and the rail damaged — as Russia hits two bridges in Kyiv.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called it an “unprecedented operation”, the seventh stage of a weeks-long campaign to wear down the bridge’s air defences. Russia said it shot down 1,441 drones overnight, the largest drone attack of the war, and struck Kyiv’s North and South bridges, cutting water and power.

Asia • Diplomacy

TRUMP IN MANILA FOR FIRST QUAD SUMMIT IN TWO YEARS

The US president arrived late on Tuesday for the first Quad leaders’ meeting in two years. He meets President Marcos Jr. on Wednesday, then heads to Shenzhen for APEC and a trilateral with Xi and Putin.

The Gulf • Tensions

IRAN DRONES TARGET U.S. RADAR IN BAHRAIN

Iran fired drones at the radar at Sheikh Isa base on Tuesday; Bahrain says it intercepted them, with no casualties. A tanker crew is unaccounted for in the Strait of Hormuz as attacks on shipping rise.
